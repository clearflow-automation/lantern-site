What we make.

Four wire products, drawn from steel rod on our own lines. Grade, size and finish are made to your drawing.

Mild Steel Wire

Low carbon steel wire, carbon 0.05% to 0.25%. Soft, ductile and easy to bend, weld and form. This is the general purpose wire most fabrication and engineering work runs on.

Typical grades
SAE 1006, 1008, 1010, 1012, 1015, 1018, 1020, 1022
Section
Round
Finishes
Drawn · Annealed · Annealed drawn
Used in

02 / 04

Galvanised Mild Steel Wire

Mild steel wire with a zinc coating. The zinc stops rust, so the wire lasts outdoors and in wet conditions. Coating weight is set to the job.

Section
Round
Coating
Zinc — coating weight to order
Used in
Fencing, chain link, barbed wire, poultry mesh, baling and stay wire, trolleys and baskets
03 / 04

High Carbon Flat Profile Wire

High carbon steel drawn to a flat, rectangular section on our flat wire line. Hard and high tensile, held to the width and thickness on your drawing.

Typical grades
High carbon · EN-42, EN-42J
Section
Flat and rectangular
Finishes
Drawn · Annealed
Used in
Conveyor metallic belt, springs, circlips, washers, wiper arms, auto cables, textile machinery parts
04 / 04

Ball Bearing Wire

High carbon chromium bearing steel, annealed in our bell furnace so it forms cleanly at your header. Made for bearing manufacturers, where the tolerance on the incoming wire decides the yield on the finished ball.

Typical grade
SAE 52100 / 100Cr6
Section
Round
Finish
Annealed
Used in
Balls and taper roller bearings, precision turned parts

How it is made

From rod to finished coil.

Every step happens at our plant. Nothing goes out for job work and comes back, which is what keeps a delivery date reliable.

01

Wire rod in

Hot rolled rod arrives at F-30, Kaharani.

02

Rod preparation

The rod is prepared for drawing on our auxiliary machines.

03

Bell annealing

A controlled heat cycle softens the steel for the next stage.

04

Multiblock drawing

The wire is pulled through a series of dies down to your diameter.

05

Flat wire drawing

For flat sections, our flat wire line holds the width and thickness.

06

Testing

Checked on our own testing machines before it leaves the floor.

07

Packed and sent

Coiled, packed and despatched — domestic or export.
