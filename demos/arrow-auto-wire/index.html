The range

What we draw.

Four families, processed from wire rod on our own lines. Grades, diameters and finish are made to the buyer's drawing — send a specification and we will quote against it.

01 / 04

Mild Steel Wire

Low-carbon wire drawn down from rod on our multiblock lines, softened in the bell annealing furnace where the application calls for it. The workhorse feedstock for fasteners, general engineering and agricultural fabrication.

Galvanised Mild Steel Wire

Mild steel wire with a zinc coat, for anything that lives outdoors or in a wet line — fencing and agricultural work, binding, and engineering applications where bright wire would not survive the environment.

Corrosion resistantCoating class to orderCoil or spool

High Carbon Flat Profile Wire

Rolled and drawn to a rectangular section on our dedicated flat wire line, held to the width and thickness on your drawing. This is the section that goes into conveyor metallic belt, textile machinery components and springs.

Flat wire lineHigh carbonProfile to drawing

Ball Bearing Wire

High carbon bearing-quality wire, annealed in the bell furnace so it forms cleanly at the customer's header. Supplied to bearing manufacturers, where the tolerance on the incoming wire decides the yield on the finished ball.

The route through the plant

Rod in one door.
Wire out the other.

Nothing leaves the floor to be processed elsewhere and come back. That is what keeps a delivery date honest.

01

Wire rod in

Hot-rolled rod arrives at F-30, Kaharani.

02

Auxiliary processing

Rod prepared for the drawing line on our auxiliary machines.

03

Bell annealing

A controlled cycle in the bell furnace softens the steel for what comes next.

04

Multiblock drawing

Pulled down through successive dies to the diameter on your drawing.

05

Flat wire drawing

Where a rectangular section is called for, the flat line holds width and thickness.

06

Testing

Checked on our own testing machines before it is allowed off the floor.

07

Packed & despatched

Coiled, packed and sent — domestic or export.
