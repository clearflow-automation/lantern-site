01 — Treatments

Every path to a
straighter smile.

From guiding a child's jaw growth to near-invisible aligners for adults — the right approach for your teeth, your age and your stage of growth.

Braces

Metal and ceramic fixed appliances — precise, proven correction for children, teenagers and adults.

Clear Aligners

Near-invisible and removable — straighten your teeth without anyone noticing.

Functional & Twin Block Therapy

Growth-modification appliances that guide a developing jaw into the correct position — most effective while a child is still growing.

Protrusion & Bite Correction

Forwardly placed front teeth, deep bites, open bites, crossbites, gaps and crowding — corrected in planned stages.

Children's & Interceptive Care

Early assessment and gentle interceptive treatment (dentofacial orthopedics) that can make later treatment shorter and simpler.

Retainers & Retention

Fixed and removable retainers that hold your result steady — the step that keeps a straightened smile straight for life.

Habit-Breaking Appliances

Gentle appliances for thumb-sucking and tongue-thrusting habits, before they push growing teeth out of line.

Records & Treatment Planning

Full clinical photographs, X-rays and study models — so the plan you're given is based on measurement, not guesswork.

Specialist Consultation

A calm, personal assessment and an honest, tailored treatment plan — by appointment only, so you are never rushed and never one of a crowd.
