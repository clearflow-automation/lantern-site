How a bridal booking actually goes

Three hours, one transformation.

The same client, the same morning. Nothing here is a stock picture.

01

Bare, and prepped

Skin read first, then prepped and colour-matched. The base is chosen for your skin, the light you'll be photographed in, and the length of the day.

02

In the chair

Airbrush or HD, built in layers and checked in daylight as it goes. Hair is set alongside, so nothing is rushed at the end.

03

The day

Finished, photographed and sent out of the door. Touch-up guidance goes with you, so hour eleven looks like hour one.
