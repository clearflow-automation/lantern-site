Your wedding day, from the chair

What the morning looks like.

One session, start to finish. No stock images used, anywhere on this site.

01

In the chair

Skin is read first, then prepped and colour-matched — to you, and to the light you will be photographed in. Airbrush or HD, built in layers and checked in daylight as it goes. Hair is set alongside, so nothing is rushed at the end.

02

The day

Finished, photographed, and out of the door. Enhanced, not transformed — you should still look like yourself in every picture. Touch-up guidance goes with you, so hour eleven looks like hour one.
