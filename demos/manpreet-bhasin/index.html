Step two

What we'll need from you

Nothing urgent — we can start with what we have. But the site is only as good as what goes into it.

  • Photographs of your work Bridal looks especially. Originals from your phone gallery — not WhatsApp forwards or Instagram screenshots, both are already blurred.
  • Your correct address We found Valley Souk, Ansals Valley View Estate — please confirm, or tell us the current one.
  • Opening hours Including which days you're closed.
  • A check of your prices We've used your published list. Tell us anything that has changed — especially the hair services.
  • The academy Course names, how many days, what it costs, and whether there's a certificate. This gets its own section.
  • How long you've been doing this Years of experience is one of the first things a bride looks for.
  • Your team If you'd like a page for them, a name and photo each.
  • Anything you're proud of Awards, training, magazine features, celebrity work.
