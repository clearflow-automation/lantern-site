Why it is charged. A colour direction is not a setting to be flipped — it is the whole site rebuilt: every ground, every panel, every border, every photograph re-checked so it still looks right and still reads clearly. That is a day's work, and it sits outside the ₹9,999 package, which covers one direction.

What is not charged. Anything inside the direction you keep. New photographs, changed prices, new services, different words, another section — those are part of the package and always will be.
