The last step

Your own web address.

Three parts, about fifteen minutes, and your website stops living on my link and starts living on your own name.

It is yours, not mine

You buy it. On your email, with your card, in your name. I never hold it and I never need your password.

In the last step you give me access so I can connect the website to it. That access lets me manage the site — it does not let me see your card, change your billing, or add anybody else. Those stay with you alone, because the account is yours.

If you and I ever part ways, the name simply stays with you. Nothing to ask me for, nothing to transfer.

Part one5 minutes

Make the account

We are using Cloudflare. They sell names at cost with nothing added on top, and they do not charge extra to keep your details private.

  1. Go to dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up On your laptop is easier than the phone for this one.
  2. Sign up with your own email address Use the email you actually read. Everything about the name — renewal reminders, receipts — comes here.
  3. Open the email they send and click the link inside This verifies you. Nothing works until it is done.
Part two5 minutes

Buy the name

I have checked these three — all free to take as of today. Tell me if you would rather have something else and I will check that too.

  • mbpeachesandcream.comMy pick
  • peachesandcreamsalon.comAlso free
  • manpreetbhasinspeachesandcream.comYour full name

Why the first one. It is short enough to say on the phone, it carries your initials the way your signboard does, and a bride can type it without a spelling mistake. The third is accurate but long to say out loud.

  1. In the menu on the left, click Register domains
  2. Type the name into the search box and press Search
  3. Click Purchase beside it
  4. Choose how many years under Payment option Take the longest you are comfortable with. It is cheaper per year and it is one less thing to remember.
  5. Fill in your name, address and phone Your real details. These are the legal owner record. Cloudflare hides them from the public at no charge.
  6. Enter your card and click Complete purchase It takes about thirty seconds. You will get a confirmation email.
